Our Story

North Star Bakery started as a small local family ran bakery with the intentions of bring fresh homemade baked goods to the local community. Through trials and tribulations North Star Bakery now is known for its fresh breads, pastries, and cakes!

Our Sourcing Philosophy

Here at North Star Bakery you will only find the highest quality ingredients sourced within the house and tightly quality controlled.
